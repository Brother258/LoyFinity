Privacy policy

Effective date: November 3, 2026. Replace the operator details and have this policy reviewed for the countries where Loyfinity operates before release.

Information we collect

When you create an account, Loyfinity collects your name, email address, account role and an authentication credential stored as a salted password hash. Business users may provide business names, categories, contact details, branch addresses and branch coordinates.

Location

With your permission, the mobile app accesses your device location while you use nearby discovery and when verifying an in-person QR check-in. Location is used to find participating businesses within your selected radius and to check that a QR check-in is near the relevant branch. Loyfinity does not request background location in this build. You can deny permission; nearby discovery and location-verified check-in will then be unavailable.

How we use information

We use account and activity data to authenticate you, operate loyalty programs, record visits, calculate reward progress, validate redemptions, display nearby businesses, provide notifications and generate business analytics. Business staff can access only the business workspace to which they have been assigned.

Sharing

When you join a business loyalty program, that business can see the customer information and program activity needed to administer the program. Transactional email delivery is handled by the configured email provider. Do not add advertising, analytics or additional data processors without updating this policy and the relevant app-store privacy disclosures.

Retention and deletion

You can request deletion inside the app or through the external account deletion page. Deletion removes the account and associated profile, session, loyalty and activity records from the application database, except information that must be retained under applicable law. Backups may retain deleted data until their normal expiry cycle, after which it is removed.

Security

Passwords are hashed, sessions use expiring access tokens and rotating refresh tokens, business data is protected by server-side role checks, and QR claims are validated on the server. No internet-connected service can promise absolute security.
